Privacy policy

Cartoon Shorts · Last updated October 26, 2026

What this app is

Cartoon Shorts is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer. It creates original animated cartoon videos and uploads them to the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

Only the owner signs in to Cartoon Shorts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's Google or YouTube account is accessed.

What Google data it uses

With the owner's permission, the app requests two YouTube permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload), used only to upload the videos the app creates.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly), used only to confirm which channel is connected.

The app does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments, subscribers, viewing history or personal information.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server other than Google's own API. No analytics, cookies or tracking are used, and no data is sold or shared with anyone.

Children's content

Videos are made for children and are marked "Made for Kids" on YouTube. The app does not collect any information from viewers.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored token from their computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Cartoon Shorts' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also follows the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's privacy policy applies to data held by Google: policies.google.com/privacy.
